A few quick answers

What can I play for free?

Solo games against Easy or Standard computer players are free and work offline. Original maple is included. The one-time Friends purchase unlocks shared-device pass-and-play and private online games. Optional finishes are separate purchases.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open the app's table shop and choose Restore purchases. Use the Apple Account that made the purchase. An internet connection may be needed to verify it. A pending purchase won't unlock content until Apple approves the transaction.

Can I move a saved solo game to another device?

Solo and pass-and-play games are saved on the device where you started them. They do not automatically sync to another device. Deleting the app can remove these local saves. Online tables use Game Center instead.

What do I need for online play?

Sign in to Game Center in your device's Settings and connect to the internet. Each online player needs the Friends purchase. A table has two to four human players in total, with computers filling the remaining seats.

How does sharing one device work?

One Friends purchase covers pass-and-play on that device. Pass the device when prompted. The next player chooses to reveal their own hand. Keep each player's cards private from everyone else, including their partner.

Where can I learn the rules?

Choose Learn the game on the home screen for ten short lessons. The move preview and accessible legal move list can help you decide what to play. If a rule seems wrong, email the situation and the cards involved after the game is over.

How do I request a refund?

Apple manages App Store payments and refund requests. Visit Apple's Report a Problem page and sign in with the Apple Account used for the purchase.

Prefer GitHub?

You can also report a bug or suggest an improvement. GitHub issues are public, so use email for personal information.
