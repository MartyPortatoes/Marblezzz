Race home together in a card-powered marble game made for friendly rivalry.

Pick a card, preview your move, and look out for your partner. Pass opponents, send them back to the start, or switch places with a jack. Bring your own five marbles home, then help your partner finish the job.

A SEAT AT THE TABLE
Play solo for free, even offline, with Easy or Standard computer players. Follow ten short lessons to learn the rules. Save your solo or pass-and-play game and return to it on the same device.

ROOM FOR FRIENDS
The one-time Friends purchase unlocks pass-and-play and private online games through Apple Game Center. Play with two to four people in total, including you; computers fill the remaining seats. Pass-and-play keeps each hand hidden until its player is ready.

A FINISH THAT FEELS LIKE YOU
Original maple is included. Optional Midnight walnut and Coastal oak theme packs change the board, marble finish, and card-back color together. Cosmetics never affect the rules or unlock Friends access.

Friends access is required for each online player. One Friends purchase covers everyone sharing a device for pass-and-play. Cosmetic theme packs are separate purchases. Online play requires Game Center and an internet connection.

Made for iPhone and iPad. No advertising or third-party analytics.
