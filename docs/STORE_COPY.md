## App listing

**Name:** Marblezzz

**Subtitle:** A little luck. All teamwork.

Race home together in a card-powered marble game made for friendly rivalry.

Pick a card, plan your move, and look out for your partner. Slip past opponents, send them back to the start, or change everything with a jack. Get your own marbles home, then help your partner finish the job.

- Play free against computer opponents, even offline.
- Unlock pass-and-play and private online games with one permanent Friends purchase.
- Invite two to four people; computers can fill empty seats.
- Play together live or take your turns over time.
- Choose a turn timer or take your time with No Limit.
- Make the table yours with optional board, marble and card-back finishes.
- Learn the rules with a short guided tutorial.

Friends access is required for each online player. One purchase covers everyone sharing a device for pass-and-play. Cosmetic packs are separate purchases and never affect gameplay.

## Privacy copy

Marblezzz saves solo and shared-device games locally. Online play uses Apple Game Center to store and exchange match data, including participant identities, game state, hands and moves. Hands are hidden from other players in the normal interface; the game does not claim server-enforced anti-cheat.

Purchases and restored entitlements are handled by Apple. The app includes no advertising or third-party analytics SDK. It does not ask for contacts, location, camera or microphone access. Support diagnostics show match status and timing, not cards or deck contents.

Deleting the app removes its local game files. Game Center match history and purchases are managed through Apple services. Apple's own privacy terms apply to those services.

## Support copy

For a rules question, consult the in-app Learn the Game guide. For a purchase issue, use Restore Purchases in The Table Shop. For an online issue, include your app version, device/OS and the redacted text in Settings → Game Center Diagnostics. Do not send screenshots of private hands during an active game.
